How to play

  1. 01 · Stake

    Set your bet in the panel beside the board. Half it, double it, or take it to the max.

  2. 02 · Rows and risk

    Pick 8 to 16 rows of pegs and a risk level. More rows and higher risk widen the multiplier range.

  3. 03 · Place bet

    Place bet settles the round and drops a ball. It bounces down the pegs into the bucket that pays.

  4. 04 · Auto

    Auto plays a set number of rounds at your stake. Stop it any time; balls already falling still land.
